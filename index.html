Why are Level and Maze disabled?

The original speed levels and mazes are not yet verified, so no options or counts are shown. Use Settings → Speed for the prototype speeds (they are not the original levels). The board is open with no mazes. Continue resumes a paused run.

Instructions

Guide the snake to the diamonds. Each one adds a segment and a point. Hitting a wall or yourself ends the run.

Keys: arrow keys or WASD while the board is focused (New game and Continue focus it for you). Space or P pauses and resumes. On touch screens use the direction buttons.

The run starts with your first direction key. The game pauses automatically when the tab is hidden; resume it yourself.

This is an early prototype. Its board size, speed, scoring and rules are provisional development choices and are not verified against any original game. Levels, mazes and bonus food are not available.
