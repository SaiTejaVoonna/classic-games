Help

Each game keeps its own options (board, mazes, colours, car, pace) inside the game.

How to play

Racer: swipe left or right to change lane, or use the arrow keys. Pass close to traffic for combos, collect coins and hearts, and reach the end of the journey. Hold the ease off button to slow down, and use + and - to change the pace.

Snake: swipe on the board, use the direction buttons or the arrow keys. Eat the fruit, avoid walls, mazes and yourself.

Each game has its own settings inside it. Leave a game with the Games button, or Exit to Games in its pause menu. Your progress is kept so you can continue later.

About

Classic Games is an early prototype of small games made with care. Gameplay is inspired by classic mobile games and may differ from the originals.

Everything is saved on this device. There are no accounts and no ads.
