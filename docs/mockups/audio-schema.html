Rules that keep it from being loud or messy
Placed in spaceEvery one-shot has a side or lane and a distance. Far sounds are quieter and duller. Waves sit on the shore side.
Rare life soundsRandom, every 8 to 25 seconds, never on top of an event, never in calm mode peaks.
One room, duckingMusic, ambience and effects share one soft reverb. Events duck the rest by about 3 dB. Calm mode is 4 dB quieter, with no hard hits.
