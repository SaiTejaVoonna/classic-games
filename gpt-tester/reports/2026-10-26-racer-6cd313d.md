## Test setup and limits

- The same Easy / Relaxed / Full settings stayed selected on retries.
- I made eight controlled attempts. None reached the finish; the best attempt reached 6,714 m of the 36 km trip, about 486 m before the first 7.2 km checkpoint. The newest attempt crashed at 1,529 m.
- The later-chapter images in this report are supplied iPhone Safari screenshots from a separate successful playthrough. They show Chapters 2–10 and the finish; they are not evidence that my sandbox runs reached those chapters.
- The live crash result panel reports distance, score, coins, and close calls, but not remaining lives or bumps. I could not record those two values for crashed runs.
- I inspected the sound controls, but this browser session did not provide a reliable way to listen to or record the music. Audio notes below are recommendations, not verified sound defects.
- No building-collapse or moving-boat event was visible in the controlled frames or supplied screenshots. Coin fading could not be confirmed from still images.
- Browser console sampling showed repeated errors from the sandbox’s browser extension metadata script. They were not from the Racer page, so I have not filed them as game errors.

## Findings

### [P2] Easy mode repeatedly ends before the first checkpoint; collision feedback is hard to diagnose

- **Where:** Controlled full-length attempts, 1,355–6,714 m; Chapter 1 and early Chapter 2.
- **Steps:** Select Journey → Easy → Relaxed → Full, start, steer around traffic, and use “Hold to ease off” when traffic bunches. Retry with the same settings.
- **Expected:** Easy mode should leave a clear reaction window and make a collision understandable. A life loss should be visibly tied to a car contact or a clear warning.
- **Observed:** Eight attempts ended before the first checkpoint. Several runs lost lives during dense traffic while the input was being changed or after easing off. Sampled frames sometimes showed the player beside or behind a traffic car without a clearly readable contact event.
- **Evidence:** Live-session screenshots are visible in the conversation; run metrics are in the table above.
- **Confidence:** The repeated early crashes are confirmed for these attempts. A collision-box bug is only a hypothesis: sandbox input latency and my timing may have contributed.
- **Suggested check:** Add a deterministic traffic seed and a debug overlay for player lane, obstacle lane, collision rectangles, speed, collision time, invulnerability window, and life changes. Test the same pattern at slow speed and normal speed. Consider a more forgiving Easy speed cap or larger warning distance if these failures reproduce.

### [P2] Traffic sprites visually touch or overlap, making lanes feel unsafe

- **Where:** Chapter 4 autumn traffic, Chapter 5 coastal truck, and Chapter 10 coast.
- **Steps:** Inspect the supplied frames with multiple cars near the camera, especially the orange and purple pair in Chapter 4 and the player/truck spacing in Chapter 5.
- **Expected:** Separate vehicles should have visible clearance and occupy distinct lanes; cars should not look as if they are entering one another.
- **Observed:** The orange and purple cars in the Chapter 4 frame nearly overlap at their body edges. The Chapter 5 truck and player also appear extremely close in projection. In the Chapter 10 frame, the group of cars ahead reads as tightly stacked. This is enough to create a “cars getting into cars” impression even where a collision is not proven.
- **Evidence:** [Chapter 4 traffic](../screenshots/user-ch04-autumn-traffic-spacing.jpg), [Chapter 5 truck spacing](../screenshots/user-ch05-coastal-close-traffic.jpg), [Chapter 10 traffic](../screenshots/user-ch10-traffic-at-1-8km.jpg).
- **Confidence:** Visual crowding is confirmed from stills; a hitbox mismatch is not established.
- **Suggested check:** Compare rendered sprite bounds with collision bounds at every lane and distance. Increase minimum longitudinal spacing or offset spawn timing; preserve clearer gaps between adjacent-lane sprites.

### [P2] Close-call reward text is too faint against the scene

- **Where:** Chapter 10 coast, “CLOSE x2” event.
- **Steps:** Observe the close-call message over the orange sunset background.
- **Expected:** A successful close pass should create a quick, legible reward moment without covering the road.
- **Observed:** The pale yellow “CLOSE x2” text blends into the bright sky and scenery and is difficult to read.
- **Evidence:** [Chapter 10 close-call frame](../screenshots/user-ch10-close-call.jpg).
- **Confidence:** Confirmed in the supplied frame.
- **Suggested check:** Put the combo in a small high-contrast toast or near the score, animate it briefly, and pair it with a distinct sound or haptic cue. Keep the road and nearby hazards unobstructed.

### [P2] Maximum speed has little matching visual or audio sensation

- **Where:** High-speed gameplay, especially Chapter 10 screenshots showing 259 km/h.
- **Expected:** Higher speed should feel faster while keeping the road and HUD readable.
- **Observed:** The speed reaches 259 km/h, but the road, roadside props, traffic, and player car still read as calm static illustrations. There is little visible change in parallax, road-marking flow, vehicle body movement, or environmental streaking.
- **Evidence:** [Chapter 10 at 2.6 km to finish](../screenshots/user-ch10-coast-2-6km.jpg), [Chapter 10 at 2.1 km to finish](../screenshots/user-ch10-close-call.jpg).
- **Confidence:** Visual impression confirmed; music and effects were not listened to.
- **Suggested check:** Increase near-field parallax and road-marking motion as speed rises; add restrained wheel blur, body roll, wind, and engine-pitch layers. Do not blur the HUD or coin targets.

### [P2] “Sunrise Coast” looks like sunset or dusk

- **Where:** Chapter 10 and the victory title.
- **Expected:** The destination and lighting should agree, with the last chapter feeling like a clear payoff.
- **Observed:** Chapter 10 uses a strong orange/pink sky with the sun low behind the hills. It reads as sunset. The finish screen calls the destination “Sunrise Coast.”
- **Evidence:** [Chapter 10 coast](../screenshots/user-ch10-traffic-at-1-8km.jpg), [finish screen](../screenshots/user-finish-results.jpg).
- **Confidence:** Confirmed naming-versus-art mismatch in the supplied frames.
- **Suggested check:** Make the final chapter visibly brighten into dawn, or rename/re-theme it so the sun position and palette agree.

### [P2] Rain reads as a screen overlay instead of weather affecting the road

- **Where:** Chapter 7 rainy city.
- **Expected:** Rain should connect to the road, vehicles, lighting, and sound so the weather feels part of the scene.
- **Observed:** Diagonal rain strokes are visible, but the road still looks dry and matte. There is no visible spray behind cars, wet reflection, or puddle response in the supplied frame. A right-edge traffic car is also partly clipped by the game stage; verify whether it is meant to be on-road.
- **Evidence:** [Chapter 7 rain and city](../screenshots/user-ch07-rain-city.jpg).
- **Confidence:** The flat visual treatment and edge clipping are visible; rain sound and driving physics were not verified.
- **Suggested check:** Add subtle road sheen and tire spray, tune rain density by distance, and keep on-road traffic fully inside the road silhouette. If rain changes grip, signal that clearly and tune Easy separately.

### [P3] Cars can read as floating paper cut-outs

- **Where:** Player and traffic cars in the supplied frames.
- **Expected:** Wheels and shadows should make each car feel planted on the road.
- **Observed:** Rear tires are drawn as tall, squared black blocks; the shadow is a separate flat oval and the body has little suspension or lean. At high speed this can look like a sprite hovering over the road.
- **Evidence:** [Chapter 4](../screenshots/user-ch04-autumn-traffic-spacing.jpg), [Chapter 10](../screenshots/user-ch10-traffic-at-1-8km.jpg).
- **Confidence:** Art-direction impression, not a physics defect.
- **Suggested check:** Use a clearer rear tire profile, small suspension movement and cornering roll, and keep the shadow attached to the tires as the car moves.

### [P3] Crash and finish summaries show different stats

- **Where:** Result panels.
- **Expected:** Finished and crashed trips should report the same set of final stats, with unavailable values explained.
- **Observed:** The crash screen shows distance, score, coins, and close calls. The finish screen also shows lives left and bumps. That prevents a player who crashes from seeing how many hits they took or how many lives remained.
- **Evidence:** [Finish screen](../screenshots/user-finish-results.jpg); crash-panel fields were read during the live attempts.
- **Confidence:** Confirmed UI difference.
- **Suggested check:** Use one result-stat component for both states and include lives/bump count in crash results.

### [P3] Mobile steering has little persistent discoverability

- **Where:** In-run HUD on a portrait phone.
- **Expected:** A player should be able to remember the steering gesture while watching traffic.
- **Observed:** The live HUD visibly emphasizes pause and “Hold to ease off,” but does not show lane arrows or a persistent swipe hint. The swipe instruction appears in the options/instructions view, not alongside the active road.
- **Evidence:** All supplied gameplay screenshots show the in-run HUD.
- **Confidence:** Confirmed from the displayed frames; the user’s successful playthrough shows that the game can still be completed.
- **Suggested check:** Show a one-time, dismissible swipe prompt at the first safe moment, then keep subtle left/right edge cues available.

### [P3] Distant world layers feel flat and disconnected

- **Where:** Chapters 2–5 and 10.
- **Expected:** Road, roadside, terrain, and horizon should read as one continuous world, with a clear sense of depth.
- **Observed:** The pastel hills and sky are attractive, but a broad pale horizontal haze band separates the mountains from the foreground world in several frames. Simple roadside houses and trees repeat in similar clusters. Chapter 5 and Chapter 10 both use a similar beach/palm/sunset vocabulary, so the final destination does not look as distinct as its title suggests.
- **Evidence:** [Chapter 2 blossom route](../screenshots/user-ch02-blossom.jpg), [Chapter 3 desert stop](../screenshots/user-ch03-desert-rest-stop.jpg), [Chapter 5 coast](../screenshots/user-ch05-coastal-close-traffic.jpg), [Chapter 10 coast](../screenshots/user-ch10-traffic-at-1-8km.jpg).
- **Confidence:** Confirmed art-direction pattern; whether any particular prop is misplaced needs motion footage.
- **Suggested check:** Blend horizon fog by biome, add distant/midground/near roadside parallax, vary set dressing and road silhouettes, and transition biomes over a short distance rather than swapping palettes abruptly.

## Environment, pickups, and audio notes

- **Cherry blossom / Chapter 2:** Bright and readable, with clear road edges. Repeated rounded trees and small houses make the route feel decorative but a little toy-like.
- **Desert rest stop / Chapter 3:** Strong palette change and recognizable cacti. “HEART RESTORED” is large and centered; confirm the message clears quickly enough not to compete with approaching traffic.
- **Autumn / Chapter 4:** Good color variation and falling-leaf dressing, but vehicle spacing is visually crowded in the close frame.
- **Coast / Chapters 5 and 10:** Water, umbrellas, palms, lighthouses, and roadside houses establish the place. Chapter 10 needs a stronger dawn payoff and distinct skyline/landmarks.
- **Rainy city / Chapter 7:** Dense lit windows create a good night identity. Rain does not yet visibly alter the road surface; the right-edge car should be checked against the road boundary.
- **Coins:** The counter increased during live play (0 → 6 → 17), so pickups were registering in the sampled runs. I could not confirm a coin fading/disappearing bug from screenshots alone. Improve pickup confirmation with a small particle burst and clean sound, and keep coin trails safely separated from blocked lanes.
- **Boats and falling buildings:** No boat movement or building fall was visible in the captures I reviewed, so these remain unverified rather than reported as defects.
- **Cars and grounding:** The existing shadows help, but tire shape and body motion can be improved to reduce the floating impression.
- **Music/effects:** The settings panel exposes Music and Effects toggles, Auto/Cinematic/Piano/Electronic/Acoustic styles, volume controls, bass, and Calm mode. I did not listen to actual playback. As a design direction, let the chapter arrangement change with the environment, layer in speed at higher km/h, and duck music briefly for close calls/crashes. Keep Calm mode’s softer mix and avoid sudden volume spikes.

## Practical tips for the current build

- On Easy/Relaxed, prioritize a clear lane over collecting every coin trail.
- Change lanes while traffic is still small in the distance; avoid last-second weaving when two lanes are occupied.
- Hold “Ease off” briefly when traffic clusters, then release it once a safe gap opens.
- Treat close-call chains as optional until collision spacing and feedback are clearer.
